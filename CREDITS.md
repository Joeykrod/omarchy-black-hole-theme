## Wallpaper

`backgrounds/1-black-hole.mp4` is built from NASA public-domain imagery, then
looped, colour-graded and composited for this theme:

- **Black Hole with Accretion Disk Visualization** (SVS14619), NASA/Goddard
  Space Flight Center — [NASA SVS](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14619), via
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Black_Hole_with_Accretion_Disk_Visualization_(SVS14619_-_2-Orbiting_a_black_hole-4K).webm)
- **Deep Star Maps 2020** (SVS4851), NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center —
  [NASA SVS](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851)
